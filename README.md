# 🛒 Blinkit Sales Dashboard

## Overview

An interactive business intelligence dashboard built using Power BI, DAX queries, and measures to analyze Blinkit’s sales performance, customer behavior, outlet trends, and product insights.

This project focuses on transforming raw grocery sales data into actionable business insights through KPI analysis, DAX measures, calculated fields, and visual storytelling.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Blinkit operates across multiple outlet sizes and locations with thousands of grocery products. The objective of this project was to analyze:

* Overall sales performance
* Customer ratings and satisfaction
* Product category trends
* Outlet establishment performance
* Fat content distribution
* Tier-wise outlet analysis

The dashboard helps stakeholders quickly identify revenue drivers and business improvement opportunities.

# 💡 Key Insights Generated

* Medium-sized outlets generated the highest revenue contribution.
* Low-fat product categories showed strong customer demand.
* Tier 3 outlets demonstrated higher overall sales compared to other tiers.
* Certain item categories consistently outperformed others in customer ratings.
* Outlet establishment trends revealed growth opportunities across specific years.
